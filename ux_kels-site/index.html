About me

I start every project with the people who'll use it.

I'm Kelsey Skye van Es, a UX/UI Designer based in the City of Gold, Johannesburg, South Africa.

I believe good design starts with understanding people. I'm naturally curious, ask a lot of questions, care about the details, and love turning messy problems into clear, intuitive and meaningful digital experiences.

With over three years of experience in the software industry, specialising in UX and UI, I currently work as a UX/UI Designer at DENM Digital, designing and building e-commerce experiences from visual design through to front-end development. Outside of my day-to-day work, I volunteer as the lead researcher on She Can Do's UX research report, exploring women's experiences in design leadership, diversity and workplace culture.

When I'm not designing, you'll probably find me behind a camera, drawing, spending time with my dogs, or hunting down a good coffee spot or pizza place.
